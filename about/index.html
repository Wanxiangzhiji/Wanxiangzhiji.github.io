About Me.

Hello, I'm 祁绩 Ji Qi, a Ph.D. researcher in Systems Engineering at Colorado State University.

My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, decision science, and complex engineered systems, with particular interests in human behavior, computational modeling, and energy systems.

My interdisciplinary background spans economic statistics, management science, industrial systems, and engineering research. I am interested in developing computational and analytical approaches that connect intelligent decision-making with real-world systems.

Beyond academia, I enjoy photography, traveling, reading, painting, classic music and exploring new places and perspectives. I am a super fan of museums and art galleries. After moving to Colorado, I’ve started my outdoor adventure, and my next goal is to climb as many 14ers as possible.
